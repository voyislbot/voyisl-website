About VOYISL

﻿High-Tech Enterprise

Founded in 2013, VOYISL delivers large-scale applications of intelligent perception, decision and control technologies across multiple domains through its full-stack self-developed core technologies. With over a decade in the automotive industry and proven commercialization experience, the company keeps investing in AI R&D, committed to extending ground mobility intelligence to emerging tracks such as low-altitude economy and embodied robotics. As a long-term builder in China's smart mobility sector, VOYISL actively expands into global markets, serving the global intelligent industry with innovative technology from China. VOYISL Group holds 200+ patents, with R&D staff accounting for nearly one-third of its workforce, and has been consecutively recognized as a High-Tech Enterprise, a specialized and innovative enterprise, and a Shanghai Sci-Tech Little Giant enterprise.
