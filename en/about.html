Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited

Founded in 2013, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited focuses on the R&D and sales of intelligent driving, intelligent cockpit and general robotics solutions and products. The Company has built VT-Navigara, an algorithms-based, software-hardware integrated technology platform comprising two universal software architectures, VT-Fundra Foundational Adaptation Architecture and VT-Bridgara Function Enablement Architecture, and three software-hardware integrated modules: VT-Sensing for visual perception, VT-Navi for multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and VT-Vulcan® for planning and control. The platform is designed to be replicable, iterative and scalable. With more than a decade of experience in the automotive industry, the Company is actively migrating and deploying its technologies to new scenarios, including the low-altitude economy, agricultural robotics and general robotics. As a long-term participant in China's intelligent mobility industry, the Company continues to expand globally, serving customers worldwide with innovation from China. As of September 20, 2026, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited, together with its controlled subsidiaries, had 121 registered patents and 49 software copyrights, with R&D personnel accounting for nearly one-third of its workforce. The Company has been recognized as a High-Tech Enterprise, a specialized and innovative “Little Giant” enterprise and a Shanghai “Science and Technology Little Giant” enterprise.
