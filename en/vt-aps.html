Product Details

A Continuous Parking Journey from Assisted to Automated

VT-APS integrates cameras, ultrasonic radars, millimetre-wave radars and inertial navigation to create a multi-source perception and control solution for low-speed parking. It covers a continuous sequence from driver-in-vehicle assisted parking and remote manoeuvring to route replay and autonomous valet parking.

The system identifies suitable spaces, continuously tracks surrounding vehicles, pedestrians and obstacles, and plans and updates the parking trajectory. For unfamiliar parking facilities, it can further extend to autonomous navigation and parking without pre-mapping or driver assistance, with sensor combinations and control strategies configured by vehicle programme.

01

APA intelligent assisted parking

With the driver remaining in the vehicle, the system identifies a suitable space and executes the parking manoeuvre.

02

RPA remote parking

The driver can operate the vehicle from outside through a key fob or smart device, supporting spaces too narrow for the doors to be opened.

03

HPA memory parking

Records a parking route during one manual drive and repeats it subsequently without the driver operating the vehicle controls.

04

AVP / AVP+ autonomous valet parking

Navigates and parks autonomously in unfamiliar facilities, with AVP+ extending the operating range between a drop-off point and a farther parking space.
