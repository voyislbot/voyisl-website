Product Details

From Perception to Actuation and Cross-Domain Integration

The VT-Vulcan® domain-controller series processes sensor inputs, plans the vehicle trajectory and issues commands to steering, braking and acceleration actuators, serving as the planning and control core of VT-Pilot® intelligent driving solutions. Multiple models support intelligent-driving functions at different levels of automation.

Beyond dedicated intelligent-driving controllers, the platform can support intelligent-driving and intelligent-cockpit functions on the same controller. Enabled by the VT-Fundra foundational adaptation architecture and VT-Bridgara function enablement architecture, it can be adapted across mainstream SoCs, operating systems and vehicle models with minimal redevelopment.

01

02

Drive-park integration

Supports driving-assistance and parking-assistance functions on a unified control platform.

03

Cross-domain controller

Combines intelligent-driving and intelligent-cockpit functions on a single platform.

04

Multi-platform and multi-vehicle deployment

VT-Fundra and VT-Bridgara support adaptation across mainstream SoCs, operating systems and vehicle models with lower redevelopment cost.

Domain Controller Architecture

Domain Controller Architecture

Central Computing

Perception, planning and actuation

An electronic control unit with one or more SoCs receives cameras, radars and ultrasonic sensors with different detection ranges, performs perception, planning and decision-making and issues commands to vehicle actuators.

Virtualization

Isolated operation and independent updates

A hypervisor architecture allows multiple operating systems and software components to operate in isolated environments and be updated independently, improving stability, security and update flexibility.

Multi-Scenario

Passenger, commercial and cross-domain

Multiple production models address passenger and commercial vehicles across highways, urban roads and parking lots, while cross-domain models carry both driving and cockpit functions.
