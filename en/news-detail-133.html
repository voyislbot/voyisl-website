Summer Dreams Take Flight | Pearl-Student Youth Meet Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited for Intelligent-Manufacturing Experience

← Back to News

Who are the "Pearl Students"?

They are outstanding, motivated young students hailing from remote and under-resourced regions. Much like grains of sand enclosed within shells, they have endured trials and been tempered to possess radiance.

Launched by Zhejiang Xinhua Love-Education Foundation in partnership with local governments and education authorities, the Retrieve-the-Pearl Programme strives to open new windows of opportunity for more young people. Through academic-and-living support, character-building education, career-guidance education and mental-health counselling services.

On the morning of July 21, this study camp officially commenced. More than 30 Pearl-Student high-school participants from Qinghai, Gansu, Guangxi, Henan, Xinjiang and other regions visited Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's Jiaxing manufacturing base.

Round 1:

At Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's patent wall, team captains assigned target patents. After the students found them, the Company's engineers unpacked the technical principles and explained them in context of application scenarios.

Round 2:

The study tour group visited the actual production lines and professional laboratories, where engineers explained the technical logic behind each manufacturing process. The students tightened screws and wired harnesses by themselves, getting up close to inspect the domain controllers.

Round 3:

Team captains from Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited stepped into the role of senior students, sharing stories of their academic journeys and career growth. Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited keeps its doors open for young people with passion and a drive to grow.

Round 4:

As study-camp activities drew toward a close, participants wrote down personal take-aways and expectations for the future on wish-note stickers.

This activity translates the complex underlying technical logic of in-vehicle intelligence into a unique, tangible experience accessible to students. Nurturing young people to discover technology, engage with it and become part of it is an undertaking Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited is committed to pursuing over the long term.
