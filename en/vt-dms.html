Product Details

Driver-State Perception Linked to Active Safety

VT-DMS uses an in-cabin camera to continuously observe facial features, gaze, hand status and head pose. It can identify fatigue, distraction, smoking, phone use, hands-off-steering-wheel operation and driver absence from the driving position.

The system also identifies camera obstruction and blocked infrared illumination that may impair monitoring and issues visual or audible alerts. In vehicles equipped with compatible ADAS functions, driver-status inputs can trigger vehicle responses such as controlled deceleration or pulling over.

01

Driver-state recognition

Combines facial features, blink frequency, gaze, gestures and head movement to assess driver state.

02

Graded safety alerts

Provides visual and audible alerts for fatigue, distraction and abnormal driving behaviour.

03

Monitoring integrity

Detects camera obstruction and blocked infrared illumination while supporting reliable low-light perception.

04

ADAS safety coordination

Provides driver-status inputs for vehicle responses such as controlled deceleration or pulling over when alerts are not addressed.

Product Details

Occupant-State and In-Cabin Safety Monitoring

OMS forms part of the In-Cabin Monitoring System together with DMS. In-cabin cameras and perception algorithms identify occupant presence, behaviour and status to support active safety and contextual interaction.

Functions can be configured around seatbelt status, child and pet presence, left-behind objects, rear-seat occupants and health status, and combined with DMS, CMS and SL products.

01

Occupant presence

Identifies occupants, children and pets in the cabin.

02

Safety reminders

Supports reminders for seatbelts, door opening, anti-pinch and left-behind objects.

03

In-cabin behaviour

Supports recognition and classification of occupant behaviour, emotion and health status.

04

Cockpit coordination

Can work with DMS, CMS, SL and the cockpit domain controller.
