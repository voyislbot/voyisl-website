VT-Navigara Technology Platform

Technology Platform

Technology is a core pillar of our business. Since our establishment, we have remained focused on technology innovation, development and iteration, with capabilities spanning the full chain of visual perception, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and planning and control.

We have built VT-Navigara, an algorithms-based, software-hardware integrated technology platform. It comprises two universal software architectures, VT-Fundra Foundational Adaptation Architecture and VT-Bridgara Function Enablement Architecture, and three software-hardware integrated modules: VT-Sensing for visual perception, VT-Navi for multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and VT-Vulcan® for planning and control. With a replicable, iterative and scalable architecture, the platform can extend proven capabilities to a broader range of applications at low incremental development cost.

Universal Software Architectures

VT-Fundra / VT-Bridgara

VT-Fundra decouples software from hardware, while VT-Bridgara uses middleware, standardized interfaces and reusable algorithms to support multiple functional configurations.

Software-Hardware Integrated Modules

VT-Sensing / VT-Navi / VT-Vulcan®

Integrated modules covering visual perception, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and planning and control can be flexibly combined by vehicle model, function and application.

Platform Advantages

Replicable · Iterable · Scalable

Supports the efficient extension of mass-production-proven technologies to diverse applications at low incremental development cost.

VT-Sensing visual perception technology is built on Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's mature automotive foundation and reused across multiple tracks, including low-altitude drones, agricultural robots and general robotics. Combined with in-house camera manufacturing, high-performance perception cameras and algorithms adapted to diverse operating conditions, it delivers automotive-grade visual perception rigorously validated through vehicle testing and supports software-hardware integrated perception requirements under complex conditions and different functional safety levels.
