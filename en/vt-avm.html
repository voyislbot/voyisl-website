Product Details

Surround Perception and Low-Speed Assistance

VT-AVM uses a four- or six-camera set and an ECU to collect images from the front, rear, left and right of the vehicle, perform image correction, perspective transformation and stitching, and generate a continuous panoramic view.

Functions can be configured according to vehicle model, including 2D bird’s-eye view, 3D panoramic view, transparent chassis, wheel-angle view, DVR and radar fence. It supports reversing, parking, turning and passage through narrow spaces by helping drivers identify nearby obstacles and blind spots.
