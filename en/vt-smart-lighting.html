Product Details

In-Cabin Illumination and Visual Interaction

SL provides functional illumination and visual interaction within the cabin, communicating vehicle status and interaction information through colour, brightness and dynamic light effects.

The system supports chase, breathing, meteor and music-synchronised effects and coordinates with AVM/RVC systems to provide dynamic warning lighting during parking and reversing.
