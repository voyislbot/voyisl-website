General robotics is a key exploration area for the Company.
Leveraging our capabilities in visual perception, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and planning and control,
we are developing perception and positioning solutions for general robotics applications. These solutions are currently at the pre-design-win customer validation stage,
while we are actively exploring applications in domestic robots, smart factories, industrial and park inspection, and warehouse automation.

Visual Perception

Multi-camera vision and deep-learning algorithms identify people, goods, equipment and passable areas in real time, providing the environmental understanding required for autonomous operation.

Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation

Fusing visual, inertial and satellite information supports continuous indoor-outdoor positioning, path planning and navigation, and coordination with campus dispatching systems.

Planning and Control

Mass-production-proven planning and control capabilities enable autonomous obstacle avoidance, smooth task execution and high standards of safety and reliability in dynamic environments.
