Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Showcases AI-enabled Visual Perception Camera at the 8th Intelligent Driving Conference

← Back to News

On October 27, the 8th Intelligent Driving Conference & 2026 Huanxiu Lake Physical AI Industry Development Promotion Conference themed “Intelligent-Connected World, Steering into the Future” was held in Xiangcheng, Suzhou. The conference aimed to explore new industrial pathways for intelligent driving to evolve toward Physical AI and share development opportunities brought by Physical AI. Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited and its controlled subsidiary Suzhou Yinyu Electronics Co., Ltd. exhibited AI-enabled visual perception cameras, and exchanged practical insights on cross-scenario technology reuse of automotive-grade visual perception technology with industry partners.

The exhibited AI-enabled visual perception camera draws on the Company's VT-Sensing visual perception capability and the VT-Navigara algorithms-based, software-hardware integrated technology platform. Built on in-house R&D perception algorithms and AI-based analytical algorithms, it enables environmental perception and target recognition across diverse complex operating conditions. Featuring replicable, iterative and scalable architectural properties, the platform enables automotive-grade visual perception technologies to be reused for low-altitude UAVs, agricultural robots and other general robotics scenarios.

Founded in 2013, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited focuses on the R&D and sales of intelligent driving, intelligent cockpit and general robotics solutions and products. It has mastered integrated cross-domain technologies covering visual perception, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and planning and control. The Company has built the algorithms-driven, software-hardware integrated VT-Navigara technology platform. It comprises two universal software architectures, VT-Fundra Foundational Adaptation Architecture and VT-Bridgara Function Enablement Architecture, together with three software-hardware integrated modules: VT-Sensing for visual perception, VT-Navi for multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and VT-Vulcan® for planning and control.

Starting from perception and rooted in the automotive sector, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited strives to bring reliable automotive-grade visual capabilities to a broader range of application scenarios.
