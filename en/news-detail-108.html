Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited CEO Brian CHEN Receives 2026 Forbes China Pioneer Innovator in Industry Development Award

← Back to News

On March 18, Forbes China together with globally-renowned growth-consulting-firm Frost & Sullivan presented the 2026 Forbes China Pioneer Innovator in Industry-Development Award. This award targets key sectors including AI, mass-consumption industries, healthcare, new-energy, manufacturing-and-service-industries. This honour represents authoritative recognition for Mr Brian CHEN’s outstanding-leadership-performance and industry-related-contributions. It also validates wide-spread mainstream-market-and-professional-community-recognition for Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited’s innovation-strengths within intelligent-assisted-driving and intelligent-cockpit-related fields.

Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited has built a “Technology-Product-Market” business-chain-system. Its VT-Pilot® and VT-Cockpit® together with VT-Sensing have achieved commercial-scale-application within domestic-markets. In 2022, Brian CHEN seized global-market-opportunities and led the Company’s team to deploy overseas-markets, achieving export-delivery of intelligent-assisted-driving-systems and initiating overseas-business-layout-work.

Against the backdrop of AI-industry-growth, he has turned strategic-focus toward low-altitude economy and general robotics related tracks, putting forward the development-strategy of “technology-reuse and value-extension” to expand application-boundaries for core-technologies. Within-several-years, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited was awarded the Second Prize of Engineering Research Achievement under the Ministry of Education Outstanding Scientific Research Achievement Award (Natural Science and Engineering Technology) for its project entitled Key Technologies and Application of Navigation and Control for Mobile Robots with Fusion Vision in Complex Environments.
