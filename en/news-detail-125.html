Dragon Boat Festival: Industry and Academia Row Side by Side | Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Sponsors Fudan University School of Management Alumni VC/PE Club

← Back to News

On June 20, the Fudan University School of Management Dragon Boat Race successfully concluded. Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited sponsored the Fudan University School of Management Alumni VC/PE Club for the second time. During the preliminary heats, the dragon boat team delivered an outstanding performance, securing second place within its group and third place among all preliminary-round participants. This result marks the club team's best preliminary-round performance in the past four years and secured its qualification for the grand finals. On the race course, team members strove valiantly and fully demonstrated the spirit of striving for excellence.

Drawing on its strong technology industry expertise and using cultural and sports activities as a bond, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited works with leading business think tanks and economics and management talent to deepen industry-academia-research resource linkages and jointly ride the tide of the new era.
