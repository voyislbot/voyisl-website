Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Named to the 9th KPMG China AutoTech 50 Ranking

← Back to News

On September 17, the 2026 KPMG China Automotive Industry Summit and the 9th AutoTech 50 Ranking Release Ceremony was held in Shanghai. Drawing on more than a decade-long expertise in the automotive sector, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited has been selected for the 9th KPMG China AutoTech 50 Ranking.

The KPMG AutoTech 50 ranking focuses on technological innovation, commercialization potential and industrial-development value. It delivers professional evaluation references for the industry to interpret trends of China’s automotive-technology sector, and is widely recognized by OEMs, investment institutions and industrial communities.

Founded in 2013, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited has concentrated on automotive intelligence for over a decade. Our technologies empower vehicles to “perceive”, “understand” and “execute”, delivering systematic vehicle-level capabilities. Leveraging this technology foundation, our product portfolio has seen deployment across various vehicle classes for mass production programmes.
