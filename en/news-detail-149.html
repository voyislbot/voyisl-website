Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Obtains ISO 26262 System Certification

← Back to News

On September 18, TÜV Rheinland Germany issued to Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited the certificate for ISO 26262 ASIL-D Functional Safety Management (FSM) System, with certificate No. 968/A-FSM351.00/26. Conducted in strict accordance with the ISO 26262 standard, the certification covers functional safety management as well as multiple stages of system, hardware and software development. This marks that Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited has established a management system meeting ASIL-D requirements and acquired corresponding product development capabilities. It supports the safety development of modules and cross-domain technology reuse for the VT-Navigara software-hardware integrated technology platform, and empowers customers to achieve efficient and compliant project delivery.
