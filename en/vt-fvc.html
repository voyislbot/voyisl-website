Product Details

Forward Perception and ADAS Integration

VT-FVC integrates a forward-facing camera and deep-learning perception algorithms in a compact product for commercial-vehicle forward-safety assistance. It supports monocular-vision-based AEB and ACC functions.

The system can detect crossing road users, road edges, drivable areas, guardrails, traffic-sign information, positioning data and special-shaped vehicles. It also supports integrated DMS, DVR and EDR functions, with camera, algorithm and control interfaces adapted to vehicle programmes.

Applications

Application Scenarios

Passenger-vehicle assistance

Forward perception detects vehicles, pedestrians, lane markings and road structure to provide visual input for L2 driver-assistance functions.

Commercial-vehicle safety

For long commercial vehicles, high driving positions and complex roads, monocular-vision-based AEB and ACC help strengthen forward-driving safety.

Forward-perception integration

Functions as an ADAS forward-perception unit and can combine with DMS, DVR, EDR and the domain controller, with installation, calibration and algorithms adapted by programme.
