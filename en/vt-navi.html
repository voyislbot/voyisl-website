Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation Modules

Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation

VT-Navi is a software-hardware integrated module for positioning and navigation. It integrates a three-axis accelerometer, three-axis gyroscope, three-axis magnetic sensor, pressure sensor and motion coprocessor, with automatic calibration and real-time fusion correcting heading drift and mitigating magnetic interference.

The module can further fuse camera, millimetre-wave radar, ultrasonic and GNSS inputs through proprietary multi-sensor algorithms and associate detections of the same object across sensors, providing more continuous orientation, motion and position information for vehicles and robots.

Integrated Sensing

Inertial and geomagnetic fusion

Integrates accelerometer, gyroscope, geomagnetic and pressure sensing with an embedded motion coprocessor running fusion algorithms inside the module.

Motion Outputs

Orientation, motion and altitude

Provides heading, roll, pitch, angular velocity, linear acceleration, air pressure and relative-altitude information for vehicle and robot motion control.

Multi-Source Perception

Cross-sensor object association

Can fuse camera, millimetre-wave radar, ultrasonic and GNSS data and associate detections of the same object across sensors.
