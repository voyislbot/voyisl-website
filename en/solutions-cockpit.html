Intelligent Cockpit

VT-Cockpit® intelligent cockpit solutions integrate optical, imaging, display and visual-perception technologies to support vision safety, in-cabin monitoring and human-vehicle interaction. The series primarily comprises camera monitoring systems (CMS), in-cabin monitoring systems (IMS, comprising DMS and OMS) and smart lighting (SL) solutions and products.

PRODUCT LINES

Product Lines

VT-Cockpit® Series

Camera Monitoring System

Uses external cameras and in-cabin displays to replace conventional exterior mirrors.

VT-Cockpit® Series · IMS

In-Cabin Monitoring System

Monitors the in-cabin behavior and conditions of drivers and occupants.

VT-Cockpit® Series

Key FunctionsCMS
  • Low-latency transmission, automatic brightness adjustment, HDR, night vision and view switching
DMS
  • Face recognition and driver identity verification
SL
  • Monochrome point lighting, footwell and door-handle illumination
CMS
  • Anti-glare, high-beam suppression, backlight compensation, software defogging and AI-enabled ISP
DMS
  • Fatigue, distraction, gesture, phone use, smoking and gaze tracking; camera and infrared obstruction detection; hands-off and driver-absence detection
SL
  • Chase and breathing effects; 64/128/256-color switching
CMS
  • Camera temperature sensing and heating, BSD display, door-open warning (DOW) and obstacle detection
IMS
  • Emotion and age recognition, seatbelt detection, height classification and child presence detection
SL
  • Meteor and music-rhythm effects; dynamic warning flashes
CMS
  • Dual-curvature display, gaze-triggered activation, sentry mode and customization
IMS
  • Rear passengers, pets, child seats and left-behind objects; door-open and anti-pinch warnings; emotion and health-status recognition
SL
  • Driving-mode-linked dynamic ambient lighting with touch panel, sound and haptic feedback

Note: The SL solutions and products under VT-Cockpit® 1.0 were originally developed by Shanghai Yuzhou prior to the Acquisition. Following the Acquisition, built on Shanghai Yuzhou's existing technologies, we developed the SL solutions and products under VT-Cockpit® 2.0 and subsequent generations through our integrated R&D capabilities.
