Intelligent Cockpit

VT-Cockpit® intelligent cockpit solutions integrate optics, imaging, display and visual-perception technologies to improve driver visibility, monitor occupants and enable human-machine interaction.

Camera Monitoring System

External cameras and in-cabin displays replace conventional exterior mirrors, improving visibility under complex lighting and viewing conditions.

IMS

In-Cabin Monitoring System

Includes driver monitoring (DMS) and occupant monitoring (OMS) to observe driver and passenger behavior and status.

SL

Smart Lighting

Provides functional lighting and visual interaction within the cabin to reinforce vehicle status and human-machine interaction.
