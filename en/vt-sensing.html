Visual Perception

Our visual perception capabilities cover multiple aspects, including optics, imaging, camera calibration, object detection, tracking and ranging, lane recognition and BEV-based perception. According to Frost & Sullivan, we are among the earliest domestic companies to adopt a BEV architecture for perception modeling. Our BEV algorithms combine inputs from multiple cameras and sensors into a unified spatial representation for downstream planning and control.

Evolution

Vision-Computing Integration

2023 · Vision-computing integration strategy

We introduced our vision-computing integration strategy, emphasizing the embedding of computing capabilities into camera modules and software-hardware integrated optimization to process selected visual data at the source.

2025 · Vision-computing integrated modules

We launched vision-computing integrated modules in which suitable perception algorithms are deployed on processors embedded in the sensing modules to perform preprocessing, feature extraction and intelligent analysis at the data source. This reduces raw-data transmission and repetitive computation and shortens the processing path from environmental sensing to downstream decision-making.
