Visual Perception Modules

Visual Perception

VT-Sensing uses mass-production visual perception technologies that integrate optical design, image-sensor selection, module packaging, ISP tuning and automotive-grade environmental adaptation. Vs-S, Vs-I and Vs-G address wide-angle surround-view, front and side-rear perception, and in-cabin monitoring respectively.

All modules can adopt a vision-computing integrated structure that combines imaging components, computing chips and perception algorithms within the module. BEV algorithms reconstruct inputs from multiple cameras and sensors into a unified spatial representation for perception, positioning, planning and control.

Vs-S

Surround and perimeter perception

Cost-efficient wide-field-of-view options for surround-view systems, parking and vehicle-perimeter sensing.

Vs-I

Front and side-rear perception

Multiple field-of-view and imaging configurations support long-distance or more detailed front, side and rear perception.

Vs-G

In-cabin monitoring and interaction

Optimized for near-range IMS, DMS and OMS applications, supporting driver, occupant and cabin-interaction perception.

Evolution

Vision-Computing Integration

2023 · Vision-computing integration strategy

We introduced our vision-computing integration strategy, emphasizing the embedding of computing capabilities into camera modules and software-hardware integrated optimization to process selected visual data at the source.

2025 · Vision-computing integrated modules

We launched vision-computing integrated modules in which suitable perception algorithms are deployed on processors embedded in the sensing modules to perform preprocessing, feature extraction and intelligent analysis at the data source. This reduces raw-data transmission and repetitive computation and shortens the processing path from environmental sensing to downstream decision-making.

Vision-Computing Advantages

Advantages of Vision-Computing Integration

On-Module Computing

Local perception and result output

Performs object detection, tracking, ranging, lane recognition and driver-state analysis inside the module and, where appropriate, transmits perception results instead of raw video streams.

Software-hardware Co-optimization

Computing, memory, thermal and power control

Integrates camera hardware, computing hardware, ISP, on-device algorithms, model deployment and automotive interfaces, using model compression, algorithm optimization, memory management and thermal/power control.

Cross-Platform

Vehicles, robots and drones

Modular vision-computing capabilities provide flexible visual-perception and deployment options across automotive, robotics and aerial platforms.
