Product Overview

CMS · Camera Monitoring System

Camera monitoring systems (CMS) use external cameras and in-cabin displays to replace conventional exterior mirrors. CMS can be adapted for commercial-vehicle and passenger-vehicle programs based on model and project requirements.

Product Details

Digital Side-Rear Vision with Active-Safety Information

VT-CMS captures the vehicle’s side-rear fields of view with external cameras and presents them on an in-cabin display with low latency, adjusting brightness to ambient light. High dynamic range, low-light enhancement, anti-glare processing and software-based dehazing help preserve image detail under challenging lighting.

The system can switch display views for turning and reversing and progressively combine safety information such as blind-spot vehicles, door-opening warnings and nearby obstacles. Installation and display solutions can be adapted separately to commercial- and passenger-vehicle programmes.

Applications

Application Scenarios

Commercial- and Passenger-Vehicle Adaptation

CMS supports both commercial- and passenger-vehicle programmes. For buses and trucks, adaptation focuses on installation conditions, side-rear visibility and applicable regulations; for passenger vehicles, it is customised around vehicle styling, mirror-area packaging and in-cabin display layout.

Regulatory Compliance and Certification

Regulatory Compliance and Certification

GB 15084-2022 and EU ECE R46

The CMS solution has passed tests under GB 15084-2022 Performance and Installation Requirements for Motor Vehicle Indirect-Vision Devices, as well as EU ECE R46 certification.
