Product Overview

CMS · Camera Monitoring System

Camera monitoring systems (CMS) use external cameras and in-cabin displays to replace conventional exterior mirrors. CMS can be adapted for commercial-vehicle and passenger-vehicle programs based on model and project requirements.

Product Details

Digital Side-Rear Vision and Regulatory Readiness

VT-CMS uses external cameras and in-cabin displays to expand side-rear visibility. Low-light imaging and image processing improve observation in rain, fog, dim light and strong glare.

AI-enabled functions such as vehicle detection and curb detection can be integrated. The system supports coaches, public-transit buses, school buses, engineering vehicles, special-purpose commercial vehicles and passenger vehicles, with installation and display adapted separately to CV and PV programmes.

01

Multi-vehicle adaptation

Supports both commercial- and passenger-vehicle programmes through adaptation to vehicle structure, installation requirements, styling and cabin layout.

02

Low-light imaging

Brightness control, HDR and image processing improve visibility in challenging lighting.

03

Installation and display

Camera position, mirror-area packaging and in-cabin display solutions are customised by vehicle model.

04

Regulatory compliance

The technology solution has passed GB 15084-2022 testing and obtained EU ECE R46 certification.

Applications

Application Scenarios

Commercial- and Passenger-Vehicle Adaptation

CMS supports both commercial- and passenger-vehicle programmes. For buses and trucks, adaptation focuses on installation conditions, side-rear visibility and applicable regulations; for passenger vehicles, it is customised around vehicle styling, mirror-area packaging and in-cabin display layout.

Regulatory Compliance and Certification

Regulatory Compliance and Certification

GB 15084-2022 and EU ECE R46

The CMS solution has passed tests under GB 15084-2022 Performance and Installation Requirements for Motor Vehicle Indirect-Vision Devices, as well as EU ECE R46 certification.
