Product Overview

CMS · Camera Monitoring System

Camera monitoring systems (CMS) use external cameras and in-cabin displays to replace conventional exterior mirrors. CMS can be adapted for commercial-vehicle and passenger-vehicle programs based on model and project requirements.

Capabilities

Core Capabilities

Camera Monitoring System

Digital side-rear view

External cameras capture the side-rear view and present it on in-cabin displays.

Camera Monitoring System

Challenging-environment viewing

Image processing, automatic brightness adjustment and HDR can improve visibility under complex lighting conditions.

Camera Monitoring System

Vehicle-program adaptation

Installation, display and regulatory requirements can be adapted for commercial-vehicle or passenger-vehicle programs.

Applications

Application Scenarios

Commercial-Vehicle Adaptation

Adapted for buses, trucks and other commercial vehicles based on vehicle architecture, installation requirements and applicable project regulations.

Passenger-Vehicle Adaptation

Adapted for passenger vehicles based on vehicle styling, in-cabin display layout and program-specific functional requirements.

Regulatory Compliance and Certification

Regulatory Compliance and Certification

GB 15084-2022 and EU ECE R46

The CMS solution has passed tests under GB 15084-2022 Performance and Installation Requirements for Motor Vehicle Indirect-Vision Devices, as well as EU ECE R46 certification.
