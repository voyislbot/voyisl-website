Delivery Models

Delivery Models

Depending on customer requirements, we can deliver solutions and products in one or more forms, combining system-level integration with reusable software capabilities on a common technology platform.

01

System-Level Software-hardware Integration

We integrate software algorithms, perception and positioning modules, and planning and control modules around one or a combination of customisable intelligent driving and/or intelligent cockpit functions, creating system-level solutions configurable by vehicle programme.

02

Standalone Software Solutions

We provide proprietary software and algorithms through SDKs or software licences, enabling customers to integrate core capabilities into their existing platforms and development environments and improve cross-platform reuse.
