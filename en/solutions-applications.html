Low-altitude applications require reliable obstacle avoidance and autonomous navigation under stringent size, weight, power-consumption and real-time performance constraints. Leveraging the VT-Navigara technology platform, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited extends visual perception, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation, and planning and control capabilities to low-altitude applications. The resulting integrated hardware and software solutions support drone logistics, inspection, emergency rescue and surveying and mapping.

Rich Application Scenarios

Addresses logistics, inspection, emergency rescue, surveying and mapping, environmental perception and high-precision positioning.

Low-Altitude Perception & Obstacle Avoidance

Built on multi-camera vision and deep-learning algorithms, the system detects low-altitude obstacles such as power lines, towers and buildings, outputting their bearing and distance in real time to give aircraft reliable obstacle-avoidance capability.

Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation in Complex Environments

Fusing vision, inertial navigation and satellite positioning, the system maintains continuous, stable position awareness and trajectory holding even where satellite signals are limited — between buildings or under bridges.

From Ground to Sky

Perception, positioning and control technologies for ground mobility are highly homologous to those of low-altitude aircraft. The mass-production-proven software and hardware stack can be reused directly, accelerating intelligent deployment and scaled adoption of low-altitude equipment.

Large farms and landscaping sites face labour-intensity and navigation challenges, representing an important extension of our automotive-grade capabilities. Leveraging the VT-Navigara technology platform, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited is developing the VT-Mow series of autonomous agricultural robots. The initial product, an autonomous lawn-mowing robot, is expected to launch in 2027. Future plans include extending these capabilities to seeding, spraying and harvesting.
