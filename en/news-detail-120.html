Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Commercial-Vehicle Intelligence Solutions

← Back to News

Compared with passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles feature larger body dimensions, more extensive blind zones and more complex operating conditions, imposing higher requirements for surrounding-vehicle perception, driver-status monitoring and low-speed-operation safety.

Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited has developed the VT-Pilot® intelligent driving solutions and VT-Cockpit® intelligent cockpit solutions, which cover diverse scenarios including forward-view, side-view, rear-view, surround-view, parking, driver monitoring and in-cabin interaction to deliver intelligent solutions for commercial vehicles such as trucks and electric buses.

Solution Advantages

  • Robust system-integration capability enabling flexible solution combinations in accordance with vehicle-model specifications
  • Custom-development capacity for project-oriented tailored solutions
  • In-house hardware-software co-development with full independent control over core technologies
  • Three manufacturing bases in Jiaxing, Wuhu and Dongguan to reinforce production assurance while balancing product performance, delivery efficiency and cost control.

Application Scenarios

Electric Buses (Public Transit / Inter-city Coaches)

Electric buses, public-transit buses and inter-city coaches mostly operate on urban and inter-city roads with frequent mixing of pedestrians, non-motor vehicles and motor vehicles. Customized product combinations are available for scenarios including bus-stop docking, turning, reversing, low-speed travel and nighttime operation.

Heavy-Duty & Cargo Trucks

Heavy-duty and cargo trucks feature long vehicle bodies, high driving positions and large turning radii. Long-haul transport and logistics operations take extended periods of time. Clear functional requirements exist for forward safety, side-rear visibility, low-speed parking and driver-status monitoring.

Product & Technology Details

Visual Perception — VT-Sensing

DMS / CMS / Reversing / Surround-View Cameras

These serve as supplementary visual-perception resources for in-cabin and surrounding-vehicle sensing. They cover driver monitoring, vehicle side-rear areas and peripheral blind zones, and supply visual data for in-cabin monitoring, lane-changing, turning, reversing, parking and low-speed scenarios. Product combinations with AVM / CMS / DMS / APA / RPA / HPA / AVP may be adopted in accordance with vehicle-model and project requirements.

VT-Pilot® intelligent driving solutions

AVM | Around View Monitor

Image stitching and correction are realized via a 4-camera or 6-camera set plus ECU. Functions include 2D bird's-eye view, 3D panoramic view, transparent chassis, wheel-angle view, DVR and radar fence. It is applicable for vehicle reversing, parking, turning and passage through narrow spaces.

APA / RPA / HPA / AVP | Parking Assistance

Integrating cameras, ultrasonic radars, millimeter-wave radars and inertial navigation, it delivers intelligent assisted parking, remote-control parking, trace-back reversing, memory parking and other low-speed assist functions for logistics parks, depots and parking scenarios.

VT-Cockpit® Intelligent Cockpit Series

CMS | Camera Monitoring System

Cameras and displays expand side-rear fields-of-view and support low-light imaging. Integrated AI-enabled functions including vehicle detection and curb detection are available. It is suitable for large coaches, public-transit buses, school buses, engineering vehicles and special-purpose commercial vehicles.

The CMS solution has passed tests under GB 15084-2022 Performance and Installation Requirements for Motor Vehicle Indirect-Vision Devices, as well as EU ECE R46 certification.

DMS | Driver Monitoring System

It conducts real-time monitoring of drivers' facial features, gaze and physical conditions. It can identify fatigue-induced eye closure, yawning, distraction, smoking, phone-use and camera-occlusion events, and supports implementation of functional requirements such as DDAW and ADDW. DMS complies with DDAW / ADDW and cybersecurity-related system requirements laid down under Regulation (EU) 2019/2144 (GSR 2.0), and supports EU whole-vehicle type-approval.

SL

It functions as in-cabin signal-warning lamps. It can be combined with 3D facial-recognition technology and infotainment-head-unit interaction. Light modes transmit vehicle-status information for in-cabin human-machine interaction and driving-safety alerts.

E-Mirror | Electronic Mirror

An upgraded alternative to conventional rear-view mirrors. Equipped with high-definition displays and low-light-algorithm-optimized rear-scene imaging. It delivers clear rear-road visibility under harsh-lighting conditions including heavy rain, strong glare and dim environments.
