ESG

Environmental, Social and Governance

We integrate ESG considerations into the core framework for sustainable development and have established a systematic management structure that is embedded in our daily operations and strategy implementation.

Environmental

Environment

We attach great importance to the environmental impact of our production and operations, strictly comply with applicable laws and regulations, and continue to promote green, low-carbon development and efficient resource use.

  • We have established environmental and occupational health and safety management systems and obtained ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification. We achieved 100% disposal of hazardous waste.
  • Solar power generation is deployed within our industrial park; as of June 30, 2026, generation reached 181,468.2 kWh. Carbon emission intensity continues to decline, from 1.0 tonne of CO₂e per RMB million of revenue in 2023 to 0.3 tonne in 2025.
Social

Social

We promote the coordinated development of compliant corporate operations and employee value enhancement to achieve sustainable development.

  • As of June 30, 2026, we had 414 employees. No incidents of forced labor or child labor occurred during the track record period, and we uphold the principles of equal and compliant employment.
  • We have obtained ISO 45001 certification for our occupational health and safety management system and established a systematic talent development mechanism that provides employees with clear career development paths.
  • We have established a comprehensive supply chain management system, signing framework, quality and integrity agreements with suppliers, and conducting quarterly assessments and system audits.
Governance

Governance

We are committed to fostering an honest and ethical business environment.

  • We have formulated the Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Management Policy, expressly prohibiting all forms of bribery, corruption, conflicts of interest and money laundering, and adhering to the principles of zero tolerance, full transparency and rigorous enforcement.
  • Through regular compliance training and publicly available dedicated whistleblowing channels (hotline and email), we systematically prevent, identify and address non-compliance.
