Technology Platform

VT-Navigara

VT-Navigara is an algorithms-based, software-hardware integrated infrastructure comprising two universal software architectures, the VT-Fundra Foundational Adaptation Architecture and the VT-Bridgara Function Enablement Architecture, and three software-hardware integrated modules: VT-Sensing Visual Perception, VT-Navi Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation, and VT-Vulcan® Planning and Control. With a replicable, iterative and scalable structure, the platform efficiently converts technologies into mass-production solutions and products and extends technology capabilities to additional application scenarios at relatively low incremental development costs.

Universal Software Architectures

VT-Bridgara
Function Enablement Architecture

Proprietary middleware, standardized interfaces, reusable algorithms and diversified software portfolios supporting multiple functions. It composes proprietary algorithms into modules supporting NOA, APA, RPA, HPA, AVP, TJP and HWP, and deploys them across operating systems, vehicle models and application scenarios.

VT-Fundra
Foundational Adaptation Architecture

A proprietary hardware abstraction layer, device drivers and underlying resource management software. It decouples software from hardware, enables algorithm development independently of hardware environments and supports migration across different SoC platforms.
