Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Secures Design Win from Chery Commercial Vehicle

← Back to News

During Q1 2026, leveraging its in-house R&D visual perception technology solutions, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited successfully obtained a design win from Chery Commercial Vehicle. The Company will supply high-performance automotive-grade 3 MP around-view camera products for Chery Commercial Vehicle’s new-model vehicles. This represents further recognition of Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited’s product competitiveness and technical adaptability within the commercial-vehicle visual perception sector by a leading OEM.

The 3 MP ultra-wide-angle AVM camera adopted within this project features an integrated lens-and-front-housing design, effectively mitigating risks of lens detachment and improving overall module reliability and service life. Four or six ultra-wide-angle cameras are installed at respective front, rear, left and right positions on the vehicle-body. They collect high-definition images around the vehicle in real time. After image correction, perspective transformation and image stitching, a 360-degree panoramic bird’s-eye-view image is generated and synchronously output to the in-vehicle infotainment unit. This enables drivers to clearly grasp surrounding environment and smoothly complete parking maneuvers, obstacle avoidance and driving tasks across complex road conditions.
