Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Secures Design Win from Chery Commercial Vehicle

← Back to News

During Q1 2026, leveraging its in-house R&D visual perception technology solutions, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited successfully obtained a design win from Chery Commercial Vehicle. The Company will supply automotive-grade 3 MP vision-computing integrated camera modules for Chery Commercial Vehicle's new-model vehicles. This represents further recognition of Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's product competitiveness and technical adaptability within the commercial-vehicle visual perception sector by a leading OEM.

The project uses a 3 MP vision-computing integrated camera module. The trained visual-perception models and algorithms are deployed on the processor embedded in the module, enabling local image processing and perception tasks while reducing raw-video transmission and duplicate processing.

Image-quality enhancement algorithms are deployed to the module to improve image clarity, brightness, signal-to-noise ratio and colour reproduction, particularly under low-light conditions.
