Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Secures Project Designation from Chery Commercial Vehicle

← Back to News

During Q1 2026, leveraging its in‑house R&D smart-vision-perception-technology solutions, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited successfully obtained project designation from Chery Commercial Vehicle. The Company will supply high-performance automotive-grade 3 MP around-view-camera products for Chery Commercial Vehicle’s new-model vehicles. This represents further recognition of Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited’s product-competitiveness and technical-adaptability within the commercial-vehicle smart-vision-sensing sector by a leading original-equipment manufacturer.

The 3 MP ultra-wide-angle AVM camera adopted within this project features integrated lens-and-front-housing structural-design, effectively mitigating risks of lens-detachment and improving overall-module reliability and service-life duration. Four or six ultra-wide-angle cameras are installed at respective front, rear, left-hand-side and right-hand-side positions on the vehicle-body. They collect high-definition surrounding-vehicle-images in real-time. After image-correction, perspective-transformation and image-stitching-processing, a 360-degree panoramic bird’s-eye-view image is generated and synchronously output to the in-vehicle infotainment unit. This enables drivers to clearly grasp surrounding-vehicle-environmental-conditions and smoothly complete parking-manoeuvres, obstacle-avoidance operations and driving-tasks across complex road-conditions.
