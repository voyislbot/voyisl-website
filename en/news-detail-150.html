VT-Cockpit® Intelligent Cockpit Solutions Secure Another Implementation Project

← Back to News

Foxtron's MODEL T2 electric bus achieved SOP in the fourth quarter. This vehicle model is equipped with Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's VT-Cockpit® intelligent cockpit solutions.

Built upon our technologies in visual perception (VT-Sensing), multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation (VT-Navi), as well as planning and control (VT-Vulcan®), the solution covers core modules including the driver monitoring system (DMS), camera monitoring systems (CMS) and ADAS 8MP camera modules. These modules support driver status and behaviour monitoring, expansion of the external field of view and forward environmental perception respectively.

This solution deployment further expands the application of VT-Cockpit® intelligent cockpit perception technologies in electric bus scenarios.
