Annual Gala of the Intelligent Cockpit Industry

← Back to News

From September 7 to 9, the 2026 International Conference of Intelligent Cockpit (ICIC 2026), hosted by the China Society of Automotive Engineers (China SAE), was grandly held in Suzhou. Hou Fushen, Standing Committee Member of the China Association for Science and Technology (CAST) and Vice-President and Secretary-General of the China Society of Automotive Engineers (China SAE), Wang Xiaofeng, Second-Level Inspector of the Jiangsu Association for Science and Technology, and Wu Xuxiang, Deputy Secretary-General of the Suzhou Municipal People's Government, attended the conference and delivered remarks. Pu Weiying, Standing Committee Member of the CPC Xiangcheng District Committee and Deputy District Mayor, presented an overview of Xiangcheng's industrial strengths. Drowsiness and distraction among drivers represent hard-to-quantify risks for global road safety. Driven by regulations including EU GSR 2.0, UN WP.29, China’s GB 47955—2026 and GB 44721—2026, DMS is evolving from simple camera capture into a compliant technology capable of continuous driver state monitoring and risk management.

On September 9, at the Key Technology Session on Human-Machine Interaction, Suzhou Yinyu Electronics Co., Ltd., a controlled subsidiary of Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited, made an important appearance. Roc Bao, Head of the Intelligent Cockpit Product Line, held industry discussions themed “The Evolution of DMS from Passive Monitoring to Active Safety Driven by Regulation.” Drawing on global regulatory-iteration trends and mass-production-deployment practices, he shared innovative pathways for DMS technologies to empower active safety for intelligent cockpits.

Early DMS relied solely on image recognition for drowsiness and distraction alerts, focusing on risk detection. With the widespread adoption of combined driving assistance, the industry requires DMS to form a perception-planning and control closed loop to differentiate driver risks of varying levels and enable differentiated responses. DMS is evolving from a simple alert module into an entry point for vehicle active safety.

Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited leverages VT-Sensing visual perception and VT-Navi multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation to build multi-channel perception and redundancy, laying a stable sensing foundation for driver monitoring. Designed on this architecture, DMS comprehensively identifies driver head pose, gaze direction and hand status. It meets the hands-off and gaze monitoring requirements under GB 47955, complies with DDAW/ADDW and cybersecurity requirements of EU (EU) 2019/2144 (GSR 2.0), and supports EU vehicle type approval.

With large AI models integrated into the cabin, DMS is evolving from identifying driver states to understanding driver states and intentions. Nevertheless, stability, verifiability and clear safety boundaries remain prerequisites for deployment. For the Company, AI is not merely a label; it augments the existing perception-planning and control pipeline, enabling the system to shift from “seeing” to “understanding” and from “judging” to “responding”.
