Tech Mulan | Paving Chinese Auto Enterprises' Full‑Stack in-house R&D Journey

← Back to News

Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's development journey exemplifies domestic Chinese automotive tech firms' pursuit of technological independence and global expansion. As Chief Architect, how has Christina Zeng helped forge the Company's technological moat and infused technology with human warmth from a female perspective?

Q1 Why Did You Choose to Join Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited?

Christina Zeng

I decided to join the fledgling Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited because I was profoundly inspired by the founder's vision: to equip Chinese automakers with domestically-controllable in-vehicle intelligent technologies. I believed joining Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited would not only fuel my personal career growth, but also let me pursue a far greater aspiration — participating in the localization of China's automotive core technologies.

Q2 Thirteen Years On, Has Your Original Aspiration Been Realized?

Christina Zeng

Absolutely, it has.

Over the past decade-plus, nearly one-third of Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's workforce are R&D specialists. As of the Latest Practicable Date (September 20, 2026), Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited (including its controlled subsidiaries) holds 121 registered patents and 49 software copyrights. The Company has been recognized as a Specialized, Sophisticated, Distinctive and Innovative "Little Giant" Enterprise, as well as a Shanghai Municipal Specialized and Sophisticated SME.

While competitors relied on technical support from overseas-chip suppliers and struggled to deliver reliable performance for China's complex parking scenarios — including narrow parking bays, irregular obstacles and extreme-lighting conditions — Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited leveraged our full-stack in-house underlying capabilities to independently optimize algorithms and rapidly iterate customized solutions, perfectly adapting our technologies to China's unique road conditions and driving habits.

Q3 In Your Opinion, What Constitutes Great Technology?

Christina Zeng

Fundamentally, valuable technology exists to serve people. It should neither remain as abstract, pie-in-the-sky concepts, nor devolve into flashy, meaningless tech stacking. For technical professionals, it requires in-depth insight into industry pain points, translating cutting-edge ideas into mass-producible, reusable engineering solutions.

Q4 Could You Showcase Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's Flagship Technologies?

Christina Zeng

For instance, our Around View Monitoring (AVM) solution has achieved domestic mass production. Leveraging its in-house R&D technology foundation, the Company has incubated CMS and a unified pre-control architecture for ADAS forward monolithic units to enable modular technology reuse, support localised custom development and enhance development responsiveness. As Chinese brands go global, tailored solutions are developed for overseas markets to support the rollout of relevant products outside China.

Q5 As a Technical Expert, What Are Your Expectations for Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited's Future?

Christina Zeng

First, we will accelerate global expansion. The Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited team will forge ahead bravely to become a key enabler for Chinese brands' global layout. My personal commitment is to lead the R&D team to consolidate our technical barriers, safeguarding the global roll-out of China's indigenous automotive technologies. Second, we pursue cross-industry technology empowerment, extending our core capabilities into emerging tracks: low-altitude economy and general robotics.

Q6 Do You Have Any Unfinished Career Goals?

Christina Zeng

To cultivate more outstanding R&D talents for Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited and the broader industry.

We adhere to a talent philosophy of "no poaching of external talents", and build an internal ladder-based R&D-training system. Our goal is to translate technical strengths into organizational capabilities, empowering every researcher to grow and excel within our platform. Ultimately, we aim to nurture a cohort of rooted, practical local-technical professionals for China's automotive-electronics sector.

Q7 What Advice Would You Give Young Women Aspiring to Join R&D Careers?

Christina Zeng

Set no limits and dare to forge ahead. In the AI era, what distinguishes humans from AI is human emotion. The technology sector has long seen the rise of female talent. The unique perspectives and personal qualities of female researchers bring vital warmth to otherwise cold technology.

Q8 How Do You Strike a Balance Between Career and Family?

Christina Zeng

For me, career fuels family growth. My son is deeply interested in my work. Whenever he spots vehicles equipped with our technologies driving on roads, he even offers "technical comments", which fills me with great pride. The seed of independent-innovation has taken root within the next-generation's minds.

In the story of Christina Zeng and Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited, we witness the sense of responsibility and commitment held by Chinese local automotive-technology enterprises.
