Intelligent Driving

Deployable to passenger and commercial vehicles, the VT-Pilot® series combines VT-Sensing visual perception, VT-Navi positioning and navigation, and VT-Vulcan® planning and control domain controllers according to programme requirements, with configurations supporting both intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit functions on a single platform. Enabled by the VT-Fundra foundational adaptation architecture and VT-Bridgara function enablement architecture, it can be deployed across mainstream SoC platforms, operating systems and vehicle models with minimal redevelopment in a cost-efficient way. The series covers progressively advanced assistance and parking functions, from around-view monitoring and transparent chassis to AEB, ACC, LCC, APA and RPA.
