Intelligent Driving

VT-Pilot® intelligent driving solutions integrate our proprietary software algorithms, visual perception modules, positioning and navigation modules, and planning and control modules, namely domain controllers. Multiple configurations support intelligent driving functions alone or both intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit functions on a single platform. As of the Latest Practicable Date, we had achieved mass production of intelligent driving solutions covering 360° around-view monitoring, transparent chassis, automatic emergency braking (AEB), adaptive cruise control (ACC), lane centering control (LCC), automatic parking assist (APA) and remote parking assist (RPA).
