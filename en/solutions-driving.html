Intelligent Driving

VT-Pilot® intelligent driving solutions integrate proprietary software algorithms, visual perception modules, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation modules, and planning and control modules, namely domain controllers. Multiple configurations support intelligent driving functions alone or both intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit functions on a single platform.
