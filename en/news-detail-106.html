Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Receives 2026 Global Automotive Supply Chain Technological Innovation Ecosystem Partner Award

← Back to News

On March 13, 2026, the 2026 Automotive Supply Chain Ecosystem Partner Conference officially kicked off in Beijing. At the concurrent Global Automotive Supply Chain Ecosystem Partner Gala, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited won the Technological Innovation Ecosystem Partner Award.

Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited attaches high importance to R&D investment and technology accumulation. As of the Latest Practicable Date (September 20, 2026), Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited (including its controlled subsidiaries) holds 121 registered patents and 49 software copyrights. Leveraging the VT-Pilot® intelligent driving solutions and VT-Cockpit® intelligent cockpit solutions together with VT-Sensing visual perception technology, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited deploys full-stack in-house R&D technologies in real-world automotive scenarios.
