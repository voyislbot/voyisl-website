Other Agricultural Robots

Large farms and landscaping sites face labour-intensity and navigation challenges, representing an important extension of our automotive-grade capabilities.
Leveraging the VT-Navigara technology platform, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited is developing the VT-Mow series of autonomous agricultural robots.
The initial product, an autonomous lawn-mowing robot, is expected to launch in 2027.
Future plans include extending these capabilities to seeding, spraying and harvesting.

Continuous Positioning over Complex Terrain

VT-Mow combines geomagnetic-inertial navigation with BEV-based SLAM to maintain positioning and coverage-path planning over unpaved terrain when satellite signals are intermittent, supporting continuous operation across complex open areas.

Coverage-Path Planning and Motion Control

Reuses visual perception, positioning and navigation, coverage-path planning and motion-control technologies developed for automotive applications, enabling continuous path planning and terrain-adaptive motion control.
