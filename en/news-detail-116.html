Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited Wins Technology Innovation Gold Award at 2026 Agricultural Machinery of the Year TOP50+ Awards Night

← Back to News

On September 17, at the TOP50+ Agricultural Machinery of the Year Awards Night of the 14th 2026 Agricultural Machinery Industry Conference, Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited won the Gold Award for Technological Innovation for its 360-degree Around View Monitor System (AVM). This represents a major industry award obtained by the Company in the new field of intelligent agriculture. The award confirms Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited’s technical strength, and reflects strong industry recognition of its strategic decision to bring cross-industry reuse of automotive intelligent-perception technology into smart-agriculture scenarios.

This event was co-hosted by Chinese Academy of Agricultural Mechanization Sciences Group Co., Ltd., Golden Century (Jiangsu) Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. and Jiangsu Dimachi Agricultural Equipment Technology Co., Ltd., and undertaken by the Industrial Information Research Center of Chinese Academy of Agricultural Mechanization Sciences Group Co., Ltd. It aims to build a platform for in-depth exchanges among government, industry, academia and research, enabling participants to discuss development plans, share innovation results and advance win-win cooperation.

AVM adopts targeted design in structure and imaging for agricultural operation challenges such as complex terrain, prominent blind zones and variable lighting conditions. The hardware consists of four or six cameras at the front, rear, left and right, together with an ECU controller. The lens adopts AA (active alignment) autofocusing with laser-welded sealing to enhance vibration-resistance and anti-adhesion performance. The cameras are rated IP69K and can withstand high-pressure washing, mud-water scouring and accumulation of straw dust.

On the imaging side, the system synchronously collects data via multiple ultra-wide-angle cameras. The ECU conducts real-time distortion correction and seamless 3D bird’s-eye panoramic stitching, and is capable of identifying targets including pedestrians, farm implements, field ridges and ditches. Supported by low-light imaging and automatic dynamic white-balance algorithms, it maintains stable recognition under conditions including mid-day backlight, strong light-and-shade contrast under tree canopies, as well as pre-dawn and un-illuminated nighttime environments. The solution also supports AI cameras. Combined with Voyager Intelligent Systems Limited’s underlying perception architecture, it delivers dual capabilities of panoramic imaging and intelligent object detection.

This underlying architecture has already obtained mass-production validation in passenger-vehicle and commercial-vehicle sectors, and can be flexibly adapted for agricultural scenarios.
