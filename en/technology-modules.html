VT-Navigara

Software-Hardware Integrated Modules

Three software-hardware integrated technology modules comprise visual perception modules under the VT-Sensing series, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation modules under the VT-Navi series, and planning and control modules under the VT-Vulcan® series. These technology modules can be composed to provide diverse intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit solutions, as well as solutions beyond automotive sectors such as low-altitude economy, agricultural robots and other general robotics solutions.

Visual Perception

VT-Sensing

Uses mass-production visual perception technologies across surround-view, front-view, side-view, rear-view and in-cabin monitoring, with image processing and selected perception computing performed inside the camera module.

  • Vs-S series addresses wide-angle surround-view, parking and vehicle perimeter sensing.
  • Vs-I series addresses long-distance or detailed front, side and rear sensing.
  • Vs-G series addresses near-range IMS, DMS and OMS applications.
  • Vision-computing integration reduces raw-video transmission and domain-controller workload.
Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation

VT-Navi

Integrates accelerometer, gyroscope, geomagnetic, pressure and motion-processing capabilities to output orientation, motion and relative-altitude information, with further fusion of vehicle sensor data.

  • Geomagnetically aided inertial navigation corrects heading drift and mitigates magnetic interference.
  • Can fuse camera, millimetre-wave radar, ultrasonic and GNSS inputs.
  • Associates the same object across sensors to improve positioning and tracking continuity.
  • Provides a positioning foundation for vehicles, aerial platforms and robots.
Planning and Control

VT-Vulcan®

Domain controllers carry computing, planning, decision-making and control, receiving camera, radar and ultrasonic inputs and issuing commands to vehicle actuators.

  • Supports multi-sensor fusion, obstacle detection, trajectory planning and collision avoidance.
  • Uses a hypervisor architecture for isolated operating systems, software components and independent updates.
  • Multiple production models cover passenger and commercial vehicles across highway, urban and parking scenarios.
  • Cross-domain controllers combine intelligent driving and cockpit functions on one platform.
