Software-hardware Integrated Modules

Three software-hardware integrated technology modules comprise visual perception modules under the VT-Sensing series, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation modules under the VT-Navi series, and planning and control modules under the VT-Vulcan® series. These technology modules can be composed to provide diverse intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit solutions, as well as solutions beyond automotive sectors such as low-altitude economy, agricultural robots and other general robotics solutions.

Visual Perception

VT-Sensing

Software-hardware integrated camera modules for surround, front, side-rear and in-cabin monitoring

Vs-S Surround ViewVs-I Front & Side-RearVs-G In-CabinVision-Computing Integration

VT-Sensing integrates optical design, image-sensor selection, module packaging, ISP tuning and automotive-grade environmental adaptation, with image processing and selected perception computing performed inside the module.

Vs-S Series

Cost-efficient wide-angle options for surround-view, parking and vehicle-perimeter sensing.

Vs-I Series

Multiple field-of-view and imaging configurations for long-distance or detailed front, side and rear perception.

Vs-G Series

Optimized for near-range IMS, DMS and OMS applications and in-cabin interaction.

Automotive Reliability

Designed for wide temperature, dust, moisture and vibration, improving image stability under backlighting, low light, glare and rapid light transitions.

High-Precision Manufacturing

COB packaging and six-axis active alignment improve lens-to-sensor positioning, edge sharpness and multi-camera consistency.

Vision-Computing Integration

Performs local perception and result output to reduce raw-video transmission, redundant processing and domain-controller workload.

Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation

VT-Navi

Improves positioning continuity under complex lighting, occlusion, adverse weather and limited satellite signals

Inertial FusionGeomagnetic AssistanceOrientation & Motion OutputsCross-Sensor Association

VT-Navi integrates accelerometer, gyroscope, geomagnetic, pressure and motion-processing capabilities, automatically calibrating and fusing measurements to correct heading drift and mitigate magnetic interference.

Integrated Sensing

Collects inertial, geomagnetic and pressure information within the module and runs fusion algorithms on the motion coprocessor.

Orientation and Motion Outputs

Provides heading, roll, pitch, angular velocity, linear acceleration and relative-altitude information.

Multi-Source Fusion

Accepts camera, millimetre-wave radar, ultrasonic and GNSS data and associates the same object across sensors.

Planning and Control

VT-Vulcan®

Multiple production domain controllers carrying computing, planning, decision-making and control

Passenger VehiclesCommercial VehiclesHighway & Urban RoadsParkingDriving-Cockpit Cross-Domain

VT-Vulcan® domain controllers receive camera, radar and ultrasonic inputs, perform perception, planning and decision-making and issue commands to vehicle actuators. Cross-domain models support intelligent driving and cockpit functions on one platform.

Central Computing Platform

An ECU with one or more SoCs carries computing, planning, decision-making and control.

Virtualized Architecture

Multiple operating systems and software components run in isolated environments and can be updated independently for stability, security and flexibility.
