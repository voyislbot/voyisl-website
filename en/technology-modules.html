VT-Navigara

Software-Hardware Integrated Modules

Three software-hardware integrated technology modules comprise visual perception modules under the VT-Sensing series, multi-sensor fusion positioning and navigation modules under the VT-Navi series, and planning and control modules under the VT-Vulcan® series. These technology modules can be composed to provide diverse intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit solutions, as well as solutions beyond automotive sectors such as low-altitude economy, agricultural robots and other general robotics solutions.

Visual Perception

VT-Sensing

Visual perception capabilities cover optics, imaging, camera calibration, object detection, tracking and ranging, lane recognition and BEV-based perception.

Multi-Sensor Fusion Positioning and Navigation

VT-Navi

Uses fusion algorithms to combine inertial and geomagnetic information with satellite and visual positioning data, supporting more continuous positioning where satellite signals are intermittent.

Planning and Control

VT-Vulcan®

Converts what the system perceives into steering, braking and acceleration commands in a timely manner. In addition to conventional intelligent driving domain controllers, it includes controllers supporting both intelligent driving and intelligent cockpit functions on a single platform.
