Corporate Governance

Corporate Governance

Transparency · Accountability · Effective Oversight

Our Company is committed to achieving high standards of corporate governance with a view to safeguarding the interests of our Shareholders. Our Company is committed to the view that our Board should include a balanced composition of executive Directors and independent non-executive Directors so that there is a strong independent element on our Board, which can effectively exercise independent judgement.

Supervisory Board

The Company does not have a supervisory board or supervisor. The Audit Committee exercises the powers of the supervisory board under the Company Law.

Board Committees

Audit CommitteeTse Kan (Chairperson), Tao Hong Ming, Lin Chih-Chi

Reviews financial information and disclosure, and oversees and evaluates internal and external audit and internal control.

Remuneration CommitteeTao Hong Ming (Chairperson), Tse Kan, YJ Chen

Formulates standards for appraising Directors and senior management and conducts assessments; formulates and reviews the policies for and proposals on the remuneration of Directors and senior management.

Nomination CommitteeYJ Chen (Chairperson), Tse Kan, Tao Hong Ming

Selects candidates for Directors and senior management, sets up criteria and procedures for selection and makes recommendations.

Internal Control & Oversight

The Company has established internal procedures to manage business operational risks, with its business operations, finance, IT and human resources departments jointly responsible for compliance with those procedures.
The internal audit department works with the finance and business departments to perform risk assessments, advise on risk management strategies, improve business process efficiency and monitor internal control effectiveness.
The Audit Committee continuously monitors implementation of risk management policies; the internal audit department reviews internal control and reports to the Audit Committee and discusses internal control issues and measures with management.
