Corporate Governance

Transparency · Accountability · Effective Oversight

The Company maintains a sound corporate governance structure with clear responsibilities for decision-making, oversight and execution, supported by robust internal control and risk management mechanisms.

Board of Directors

The Board is responsible for corporate strategy, major business decisions and oversight of management.

Supervisory Board

The Company does not have a supervisory board or supervisor. The Audit Committee exercises the powers of the supervisory board under the Company Law.

Board Committees

Audit CommitteeTse Kan (Chairperson), Tao Hong Ming, Lin Chih-Chi

Reviews financial information and disclosure, and oversees and evaluates internal and external audit and internal control.

Remuneration CommitteeTao Hong Ming (Chairperson), Tse Kan, YJ Chen

Formulates and reviews remuneration policy and structure, and assesses the performance and remuneration of directors and senior management.

Nomination CommitteeYJ Chen (Chairperson), Tse Kan, Tao Hong Ming

Reviews Board composition, diversity and nomination policy, and recommends director candidates to the Board.

Internal Control & Oversight

The Audit Committee continuously oversees the effectiveness of risk management and internal control.
The internal audit function reviews internal control and reports findings to the Audit Committee.
Compliance training, reporting channels and oversight mechanisms support continuous improvement in governance transparency.

Corporate Governance — Director and committee information is based on the prospectus as of the Latest Practicable Date, September 20, 2026, and is subject to the Company’s formal disclosures.
